## Separate output lanes

### Research baseline

The model-only research lane contains state, trajectory, forecast distributions, peers, analogues and explanations.

It uses zero WEO provider-projection rows.

### Production benchmark

Production risk score, risk category and production crisis probability may be displayed beside research outputs as a read-only benchmark.

They are not blended into the research state or forecast.

### Provider scenarios

WEO 2026–2031 projections remain a separate scenario-availability lane. They are not observations, transition targets, calibration outcomes or realizations.

### Crisis overlay

The Phase 4 state-based crisis overlay is rejected and is not served.

Every country record contains:

- `research_crisis_overlay_status = not_approved`;
- `research_crisis_probability = null`.

Only the unchanged production crisis probability may appear in the production benchmark panel.

## Read-only shadow viewer

A separate Streamlit entry point was added:

`research_shadow_app.py`

The viewer:

- verifies the frozen manifest and file hashes;
- labels the interface as research/not production;
- keeps production and research outputs in separate panels;
- exposes uncertainty, future peer movement, explanations and analogues;
- suppresses the rejected research crisis overlay;
- displays provider projections only as a separate scenario-availability notice;
- performs no network writes and no production artifact writes.

The viewer has not been deployed.

## Prospective confirmation ledger

The initial ledger locks one record per country/horizon, including:

- forecast batch and record identifiers;
- issue date and source cutoff;
- state origin and target years;
- forecast quality and information support;
- calibrated movement radii;
- coordinate-forecast hash;
- `realization_status = pending`;
- earliest eligible evaluation date;
- blank realized-state and scoring fields.

The scoring harness is:

`src/forecasting/phase5_prospective.py`

It is append-only and refuses:

- provider projections presented as realized outcomes;
- duplicate realization records;
- incomplete 96-dimensional realized states.

When eligible future observed data arrive, it can calculate state RMSE/MAE, improvement over no change and 50%/80%/95% region coverage without altering the frozen forecast.
